Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Josh-Tim Allen built the Athletic Games app (the “App”). The App is free to download and play. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its online services collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information. Some of it is collected directly by us, and some is collected by the third-party services the App uses (see “Third-Party Services” below).

  • Sign-in and account identifiers: When you sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), we receive a player identifier and display name from that platform so your progress and online results can be linked to you. We do not receive your password.
  • Game progress and save data: Your progress, unlocks, currency, athlete and team customization, and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in, a backup of this progress is saved to Google Play Saved Games or Game Center saved games so it can be restored.
  • Online play and tournaments: When you play online or in an online tournament, your display name, team and appearance choices, and race results are sent to our game servers so other players can compete with you. Connecting to these servers necessarily shares your IP address with them.
  • Usage and analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to record in-game events, such as opening the game, selecting and starting events, and completing events. These events include the discipline, play type, whether a race was a tournament, and the gender and event type selected. This helps us understand which features are used and improve the game. It does not include your name or contact details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: When the App crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report that may include your device model, operating system version, app version, the time of the crash, and the game state (for example, the event being played). Crash reports are used only to find and fix problems.
  • Remote configuration and app verification: The App fetches settings from Firebase Remote Config (for example, server status and feature toggles). Firebase App Check and Firebase Installations use an app-instance identifier and device attestation to confirm requests come from the genuine App.
  • Advertising identifiers: The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address to show ads and measure ad performance.
  • Purchase information: In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can deliver the item. We never receive or store your payment card details.
How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide, run, and restore your progress in the game;
  • Enable online play, tournaments, and leaderboards;
  • Show ads and, where you have consented, personalize them;
  • Measure which features are used and improve the game;
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and errors;
  • Prevent abuse and confirm that requests come from the genuine App.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each one processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google (Google Play Games, Google Play Saved Games, Google Play Billing)
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check, Installations)
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (consent)
  • Apple (Game Center, App Store, App Tracking Transparency)
  • Unity (in-app purchasing and services)
Advertising and Your Choices

When you first open the App, you may be shown a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform. On iOS, you may also be asked whether to allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice at any time in the App’s settings or in your device’s privacy settings (for example, by turning off ad personalization in your Google account or limiting ad tracking in iOS Settings). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Data Storage and Retention

Game progress is stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in the saved-game service of your platform (Google Play or Game Center). Online play data is kept on our game servers only as long as needed to run tournaments and leaderboards. Crash reports and analytics data are retained by Firebase according to its standard retention settings. You can delete your local progress by uninstalling the App, and you can delete your platform saved game through your Google Play or Game Center account settings.

Permissions

The App requests access to the internet so it can connect to our servers and third-party services. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information, so we use commercially reasonable means to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to Other Sites

The App and this website may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. These external sites are not operated by us, so we strongly advise you to review the privacy policy of each site you visit.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent for personalized advertising. To make a request, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
