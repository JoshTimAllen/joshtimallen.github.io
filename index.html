About

I'm a software developer from Jamaica with a strong focus on games, and multiplayer is what I love most.

I've built all kinds of games, but there's something exciting about connecting players through competition and teamwork. I specialise in Unity and write mostly in C#, making gameplay that's fun and scales.

My first commercial title, Athletic Games, is on Google Play and the App Store and has passed 100,000 installs. That milestone keeps me building.
